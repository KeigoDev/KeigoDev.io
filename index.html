About

Background

I'm a Business Planning & Analytics Officer at Citi, based out of Taguig, where I sit between operations, procurement, and technology — turning raw supplier and payment data into dashboards that leadership actually relies on for decisions.

My work lives where business analysis meets data science: understanding what a stakeholder needs, then building the pipeline and visualization that gets them there.

Day to day that means building and maintaining reporting across Tableau, MicroStrategy, Quantum GEP, Zephyr, and SAP Fieldglass for APAC Balance of Trade and Procure-to-Pay operations, automating pipelines with SQL, and governing supplier master data so the numbers people see are numbers they can trust.

I studied Information Technology at STI College Ortigas-Cainta, and outside of work I build data science projects — from NLP language modeling to customer experience dashboards — to keep sharpening the technical half of that equation.
